The Loyal Mongoose (Indian Folktale):


"The Loyal Mongoose" is a famous Panchatantra tale about a farmer's wife who impulsively kills their pet mongoose after seeing its bloody mouth, assuming it harmed her baby. She discovers too late that the animal killed a snake to protect the child, highlighting the dangers of acting in haste.

Key Elements of the Folktale:

The Characters:

A farmer, his wife, their newborn son, and a pet mongoose.

The Conflict:

The wife leaves the baby with the mongoose, only to return and see the animal's mouth covered in blood.

The Hasty Action:

Assuming the mongoose killed her son, she throws her basket of vegetables at it and kills it.

The Truth:

She finds her baby sleeping safely, with a dead, torn-up snake on the floor, realizing the mongoose was its savior.

The Moral:

"Think before you act" or "Do not make decisions in haste".
